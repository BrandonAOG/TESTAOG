Plain-language summary of NEC 2023 314.16 — not the official code text. Verify with the adopted code & AHJ.
314.16 — GENERAL REQUIREMENT
Boxes and conduit bodies must be an approved size with free space for every conductor inside. The box volume from (A) can never be less than the fill total from (B). Conduit bodies are sized by (C).
314.16 — WHAT IT DOESN'T COVER
Does not apply to terminal housings that come with motors or generators (see 430.12). Boxes with 4 AWG or larger conductors must also meet 314.28. Outlet and device boxes must also meet the depth rules in 314.24.
314.16(A) — BOX VOLUME
Volume = all assembled sections plus plaster rings, domed covers, extension rings, etc. — but only if they're marked with their volume or made from boxes listed in Table 314.16(A).
Barriers: the volume is split between the spaces they create. An unmarked barrier uses 0.5 in³ if metal, 1.0 in³ if nonmetallic.
314.16(A)(1) — STANDARD BOXES
Standard boxes that aren't marked with a volume use the volume in Table 314.16(A).
314.16(A)(2) — OTHER BOXES
Boxes of 100 in³ or less that aren't in the table, and all nonmetallic boxes, must be durably and legibly marked with their volume by the manufacturer. A table box that's actually larger than the table volume may be marked with its larger volume.
314.16(B) — FILL CALCULATION
Add together every allowance from (B)(1) through (B)(6) that applies. No allowance for small fittings like locknuts and bushings. With a barrier, figure each space separately.
314.16(B)(1) — CONDUCTOR FILL
• Comes from outside the box and is spliced or terminated inside → 1
• Passes through with no splice or termination → 1
• Unbroken loop or coil at least twice the 300.14 free-conductor length (≥12") → 2
• Conductor with no part leaving the box (pigtail, internal jumper) → 0
Use Table 314.16(B)(1) for the volume of each.
Exception: EGCs, and/or up to four fixture wires smaller than 14 AWG, may be left out when they come into the box from a domed luminaire or similar canopy and terminate in that box.
314.16(B)(2) — CLAMP FILL
One or more internal cable clamps (factory or field supplied) → one allowance based on the largest conductor present in the box. A cable connector whose clamp is outside the box → no allowance.
314.16(B)(3) — SUPPORT FITTINGS FILL
One or more luminaire studs or hickeys → one allowance for each type of fitting, based on the largest conductor present in the box. (Stud + hickey = 2 allowances.)
314.16(B)(4) — DEVICE OR EQUIPMENT FILL
Each yoke or strap holding one or more devices → double allowance, based on the largest conductor connected to the device(s) on that yoke. A device or utilization equipment wider than a single 2" device box → double allowance for each gang needed to mount it.
314.16(B)(5) — EQUIPMENT GROUNDING CONDUCTOR FILL
Up to four EGCs entering the box → one allowance based on the largest EGC entering. Each additional EGC beyond four → ¼ allowance, based on the largest EGC entering.
Example: 6 × #12 EGCs = 1 + ¼ + ¼ = 1.5 × 2.25 = 3.375 in³.
314.16(B)(6) — TERMINAL BLOCK FILL
Each terminal block assembly → one allowance based on the largest conductor(s) terminated to that assembly.
314.16(C)(1) — CONDUIT BODIES, GENERAL
Conduit bodies with 6 AWG or smaller (other than short-radius bodies) need a cross-section at least twice that of the largest conduit/tubing they can attach to. Max conductors = what Chapter 9, Table 1 allows for that conduit.
314.16(C)(2) — CONDUIT BODIES WITH SPLICES / DEVICES
Only conduit bodies marked with their volume may contain splices, taps, or devices. Max conductors are figured using 314.16(B). The body must be rigidly and securely supported.
314.16(C)(3) — SHORT-RADIUS CONDUIT BODIES
Capped elbows, service-entrance elbows, etc. (6 AWG or smaller) are only for pulling — no splices, taps, or devices. Must be an approved size with free space for all conductors.
