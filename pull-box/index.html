📋 NEC 2023 — 314.28 Quick Reference
Plain-language summary of NEC 2023 314.28 — not the official code text. Verify with the adopted code & AHJ.
314.28 — GENERAL
Boxes and conduit bodies used as pull or junction boxes must meet (A) through (E). Exception: terminal housings that come with motors follow 430.12 instead.
314.28(A) — MINIMUM SIZE
Applies to raceways with insulated conductors 4 AWG or larger, and cables with conductors 4 AWG or larger. When a dimension is based on raceway diameter, use the trade size as the number (e.g. 2" conduit = 2 in.).
314.28(A)(1) — STRAIGHT PULLS
Box (or conduit body) length must be at least 8 × the trade size of the largest raceway.
314.28(A)(2) — ANGLE OR U PULLS, OR SPLICES
• Distance from each raceway entry to the opposite wall ≥ 6 × the largest raceway in a row, plus the sum of the diameters of all the other raceway entries in that same row on the same wall.
• Figure each row separately; the row giving the largest distance governs.
• Raceway entries that enclose the same conductor must be at least 6 × the larger raceway apart. Conductors spliced or tapped in the box are not the same conductor in and out, so this spacing doesn't apply to them (the wall-to-wall rule still does).
• Cables: use the smallest trade size raceway that would be required for the number and size of conductors in the cable.
Exception: an entry in the wall opposite a removable cover may use the one-wire-per-terminal distance from Table 312.6(A) for the wall-to-cover distance.
314.28(A)(3) — SMALLER DIMENSIONS
Listed boxes or conduit bodies smaller than (A)(1)/(A)(2) are allowed when the conductor fill is less than Chapter 9, Table 1 allows. Listed conduit bodies with a curve radius per Chapter 9, Table 2 (one-shot / full-shoe bender) may be used and must be marked as evaluated for this. If the listing limits conductors, the box is permanently marked with the max number and size; other combinations can't exceed that marked conductor area (product standards assume XHHW unless stated).
314.28(B) — CONDUCTORS IN LARGE BOXES
If any dimension is over 6 ft, all conductors must be cabled or racked in an approved manner.
314.28(C) — COVERS
Every pull box, junction box and conduit body needs a cover that fits its construction and conditions. Metal covers must be grounded per 250.110.
314.28(D) — PERMANENT BARRIERS
With permanent barriers installed, each section is treated as a separate box.
314.28(E) — POWER DISTRIBUTION BLOCKS
Allowed only in boxes over 100 in³ (EGC terminal bars are allowed in smaller boxes). Blocks must be listed; box must also meet the block's installation instructions for size; terminal bending space per 312.6; no exposed uninsulated live parts (cover on or off); through-conductors must leave the block terminals unobstructed.
Minimum wire-bending space — 1 wire per terminal column (used by the 314.28(A)(2) exception).

TRADE SIZEFITTING WIDTH (in)DEFAULT
STRAIGHT PULL
Length = 8 × largest raceway
Ex: 3" in, 3" out → 8 × 3 = 24 in
ANGLE / U PULL OR SPLICE — PER ROW
Distance = 6 × largest in row + sum of all others in that row
Ex: Left wall row: 3", 2", 2" → (6 × 3) + 2 + 2 = 22 in to the right wall
SPLICES — EVEN ON OPPOSITE WALLS
When conductors are spliced in the box, the angle/U/splice rule applies, not the straight-pull rule
Ex: 3" left → 3" right, spliced → 6 × 3 = 18 in (not 8 × 3 = 24 in)
MULTIPLE ROWS
Do the math for each row; the largest row result is the requirement for that wall.
Ex: Row 1 = 22 in, Row 2 = (6 × 2) + 1.5 = 13.5 in → use 22 in
SAME-CONDUCTOR ENTRIES
Spacing between the two raceways the conductors pass through = 6 × the larger
Ex: 3" left ↔ 3" top → 6 × 3 = 18 in apart
Spliced or tapped in the box: not the same conductor → no spacing requirement between those entries
BACK WALL OPPOSITE REMOVABLE COVER
Depth may use Table 312.6(A), 1 wire per terminal
Ex: 4/0 Cu → 4 in instead of 6 × raceway
