📋 Formulas & Tables
AMPS
1Φ
A = kVA × 1000 ÷ V
3Φ
A = kVA × 1000 ÷ (1.732 × V)
A = amps · kVA = kilovolt-amps · V = system volts · 1Φ = single phase · 3Φ = three phase
kW / kVA
kW = kVA × PF
kVA = kW ÷ PF
kW = real power (kilowatts) · kVA = apparent power (kilovolt-amps) · PF = power factor
V-DROP
1Φ / DC
VD = 2 × A × R × L ÷ 1000
3Φ
VD = 1.732 × A × R × L ÷ 1000
K formula
VD = 2 × K × L × I ÷ CM
K, 3Φ
VD = 1.732 × K × L × I ÷ CM
VD = volts dropped · A or I = amps · L = one-way length in feet
R = Ω per 1,000 ft (Ch. 9 Table 8, 75°C) ÷ parallel sets
K = 12.9 copper / 21.2 aluminum · CM = circular mils (Table 8)
% DROP
%VD = VD ÷ system volts × 100
%VD = percent voltage drop · VD = volts dropped
Target: ≤ 3% per circuit, ≤ 5% total (210.19 / 215.2 Info. Notes)
OHM'S LAW
Volts
V = I × R
Amps
I = V ÷ R
Ohms
R = V ÷ I
Watts
P = V × I
P = I² × R
P = V² ÷ R
V = volts · I = amps · R = ohms (resistance) · P = watts (power)
GEN AMPS
120/240 1Φ
A = kW × 1000 ÷ 240
3Φ
A = kW × 1000 ÷ (1.732 × V × 0.8)
A = amps · kW = generator rating · V = system volts (3Φ)
1Φ uses PF 1.0 · 3Φ uses PF 0.8
