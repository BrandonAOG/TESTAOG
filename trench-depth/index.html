Plain-language summary of NFPA 54 (2024) and NFPA 58 (2024) — not the official code text. Verify with the adopted code & AHJ.
NFPA 54 7.1.2.1 — NATURAL GAS COVER
Underground piping: 12" minimum cover. Increase to 18" where external damage is likely. Where 12" can't be provided, install the piping in conduit.
NFPA 54 7.1.2 — PROTECTION
Protect against heavy vehicle traffic and settling. Protect piping where it passes through flower beds, shrub beds and other cultivated areas where damage is reasonably expected.
NFPA 54 7.1.2.2 / 7.1.2.3 — TRENCH & BACKFILL
Grade the trench so the pipe has firm, continuous bearing on the bottom. If flooding the trench to settle backfill, don't float the pipe off its bearing.
NFPA 54 7.1.3 — STEEL PIPE
Galvanizing alone isn't adequate underground. Use corrosion-resistant material, factory coating (coat fittings & joints), or cathodic protection. Insulating fitting where dissimilar metals join underground.
NFPA 54 7.1.7 — PLASTIC (PE) PIPE
Outdoors, underground only — terminate above ground with an anodeless riser. Bury a continuous tracer: a product made for it, 14 AWG min. insulated copper, or UL 2989 tracer wire; provide access from above ground, or bring one end up at a wall or riser.
NFPA 58 6.11.3.13 — LP METALLIC PIPE
12" minimum cover; 18" where external damage is likely. If 12" can't be maintained: in conduit or bridged (shielded). Account for vehicles under driveways (6.11.3.14). Steel needs cathodic protection (6.11.3.15–16); dielectric fittings at building penetrations, never underground.
NFPA 58 6.11.5 — LP PLASTIC PIPE
Outdoors, underground only. 12" cover; 18" where damage is likely; conduit or bridged if 12" can't be met. Anodeless risers — horizontal part buried at least 12". Tracer wire 14 AWG min. (or tape), one end above ground at a wall or riser, and not touching the pipe.
FLORIDA NOTE
Natural gas in Florida is enforced under the Florida Building Code – Fuel Gas, which can differ from NFPA 54 (for example, a shallower depth for an individual line to an outdoor appliance where approved). Confirm with the AHJ before using anything less than 12".
Plain-language summary of NEC 2023 300.5 — not the official code text.
NOTE 1 — HOW COVER IS MEASURED
From the top of the cable, conduit or raceway to the top of finished grade, concrete or similar cover.
NOTE 2 — CONCRETE-ENCASED RACEWAYS
A raceway approved for burial only when encased in concrete needs a concrete envelope at least 2" thick all around.
NOTE 3 — RISING FOR TERMINATIONS
Shallower depth is OK where the cable or conductors come up for terminations or splices, or where access is otherwise needed.
NOTE 4 — SHALLOWEST ALLOWED
If a Column 1–3 wiring method carries a Column 4 or 5 circuit, the shallower depth may be used.
NOTE 5 — SOLID ROCK
Where rock prevents the required depth: metal raceway or nonmetallic raceway permitted for direct burial, covered by at least 2" of concrete down to the rock.
NOTE 6 — DIRECT-BURIED EMT
EMT buried directly in the earth must meet 358.10 (corrosion protection approved for the soil conditions).
FOOTNOTE 1 — LISTED LOW-VOLTAGE LIGHTING
Column 5, all other locations: less than 6" is allowed if the installation instructions of a listed low-voltage lighting system say so.
FOOTNOTE 2 — POOL / SPA / FOUNTAIN LIGHTING
Column 5, all other locations: 6" is allowed for pool, spa & fountain lighting in nonmetallic raceway, 30 V max, when part of a listed low-voltage lighting system.
300.5(D)(1) — EMERGING FROM GRADE
Direct-buried cables (Columns 1 & 4) coming up out of the ground need an enclosure or raceway from the required cover depth up to 8 ft above grade — never more than 18" below grade required.
300.5(D)(3) — WARNING RIBBON
Service conductors not concrete-encased and buried 18" or deeper need a warning ribbon in the trench at least 12" above them.
300.5(D)(4) — PHYSICAL DAMAGE
Where subject to damage: EMT, RMC, IMC, RTRC-XW, Schedule 80 PVC or equivalent.
300.5(F) — BACKFILL
No large rocks, paving, cinders, sharp or corrosive material against the raceway or cable. Use sand/select fill, running boards or sleeves where needed.
300.5(G) / (H) — SEALS & BUSHINGS
Seal or plug raceways where moisture could reach live parts (and spares). Bushing or bushed fitting where a raceway ends underground and cable continues direct-buried.
300.5(I) — SAME CIRCUIT TOGETHER
All conductors of a circuit — including neutral and EGC — in the same raceway/cable or close together in the same trench.
300.5(B), (C), (J), (K)
Underground raceways are wet locations. Cable under a building must be in a raceway (MI/MC exceptions). Allow for settling (S-loops, expansion fittings). Directional boring: cable/raceway approved for it.
